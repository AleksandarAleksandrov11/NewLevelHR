## What the directive changes
- Pay range in job ads or before the first interview; no questions about pay history
- Right of employees to information about pay levels for comparable work
- Gender pay gap reporting phased in by company size
- Objective, gender-neutral criteria for pay and progression

## What it means for a startup or SME
- Guesswork pay stops working: salary bands become mandatory in practice
- Job architecture: roles, levels, criteria
- Interview and job-ad guidelines (what not to ask)

## A preparation checklist
- Map roles and levels
- Define salary bands with criteria
- Rewrite job ads and interview guides
- Set up a process for pay information requests
