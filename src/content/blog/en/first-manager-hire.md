## The promotion nobody prepares for
- Technical excellence earns the title; leading people is a different craft
- What changes on day one of being a lead

## The hidden costs
- Delayed feedback and unmanaged conflict
- The team's strongest people leave first
- The new lead burns out doing both jobs

## What the first manager needs
- Clear expectations for the role, in writing
- A small set of tools: 1:1s, feedback, delegation, goal setting
- A coach in their corner for the first hard conversations
